📈 Twitter / X Growth Playbook
01
Reply first, post second. Spend 20 mins every morning replying to big accounts in #WebDesign and #FreelanceLife. This gets you seen by their audience for free.
02
Pin your best tweet. Pin a tweet that explains NeilHunter clearly with a signup link. Every new follower sees it first.
03
Post threads weekly. Threads like "How I find 10 clients in 5 minutes" outperform single tweets 5x. End every thread with a CTA to sign up.
04
Post real numbers. "I just found 13 leads in Lagos in 90 seconds" performs 10x better than vague claims. Be specific and show screenshots.
05
Use the "Comment HUNT" trick on Twitter too. "Comment HUNT and I'll DM you free access" drives massive replies and algorithm boost.
💼 LinkedIn Growth Playbook
01
Post long-form stories, not ads. LinkedIn rewards posts that start with a hook and tell a story. "A freelancer told me something that changed how I think about clients..." — then pitch NeilHunter at the end.
02
Use the comment-bait trick. End every post with "Comment 'HUNT' and I'll send you free early access." LinkedIn's algorithm explodes when posts get comments.
03
Connect with your target audience daily. Add 10–20 freelancers, web designers, and agency owners per day. Send a personalised connection note mentioning NeilHunter.
04
Share build-in-public milestones. "NeilHunter just hit 100 signups" posts perform extremely well on LinkedIn. People love progress stories.
05
Post at peak times. Best times: Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am (morning commute) and 12–1pm (lunch). Avoid weekends on LinkedIn.
