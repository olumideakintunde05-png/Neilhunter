Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 26, 2026 · Effective immediately
Summary: We collect only what we need to run NeilHunter. We never sell your data. You can delete your account and all your data at any time.

1. Who We Are

NeilHunter ("we", "us", "our") is a lead generation platform that helps freelancers and web designers find local businesses that need their services. Our website is located at neilhunter.name.ng.

2. What Data We Collect

When you create an account and use NeilHunter, we collect:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication)
  • Usage data — number of searches performed each day, plan type (FREE, Starter, or Pro)
  • Payment information — plan selected, payment method (card or bank transfer), and payment reference number. We do NOT store your card details — payments are processed by Paystack
  • Device information — browser type and device type for improving user experience

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To enforce your plan limits (search quotas)
  • To process and verify your payments
  • To send you account-related emails (verification, plan updates)
  • To improve the platform

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to any third party.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase Firestore, located in the United States. Firebase complies with GDPR and industry-standard security practices. Your password is never stored in plain text.

5. Third-Party Services

NeilHunter uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and database storage
  • Paystack — payment processing
  • Google Maps API — business search results
  • OpenAI — AI-powered business analysis
  • EmailJS — transactional email notifications

Each of these services has their own privacy policies which govern how they handle data.

6. Cookies

NeilHunter uses minimal browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) to remember your login session, theme preference, and plan information.We use Google Analytics to understand how users interact with our platform. We may also use Meta (Facebook) Pixel for advertising purposes.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at neilhuntersupport@gmail.com.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

NeilHunter is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or through a notice on the platform.
